# Assignment grading

Each assignment is scored on three criteria. Each criterion is worth 0 to 3
points, for 9 points total.

## Criterion 1: Submission — Did the work arrive in a form I can grade?

Points | Description
-|-
3      | The answers and files are submitted as requested.
2      | Something small was off but I could still find and grade it.
1      | Something got in the way of grading; I had to hunt for your work.
0      | Nothing was submitted.

This criterion is not about how good the work is. It is about whether it arrived
in the shape the handout asked for. Following the directions here costs you
almost nothing and makes grading fast enough that you get your work back
quickly.

## Criterion 2: Completeness — was every problem attempted?

Points | Description
-|-
3      | Everything was attempted. A small oversight is fine.
2      | Something significant was skipped.
1      | Multiple significant items were skipped.
0      | Nothing was submitted.

This is a quick check for an *attempted*, not for a *correct* answer. A serious
attempt that does not work counts as attempted. A blank file does not.

If you get stuck, submit what you have with a comment describing where you got
stuck and what you tried. That is worth real points here, and it tells me
something I can actually help with.

## Criterion 3: Correctness — does the work show you understand the core concepts?

Points | Description
-|-
3      | Yes.
2 	    | A core concept is misunderstood.
1 	    | Multiple key understandings are missing.
0 	    | Little or no understanding of the core concepts is evident.

This is about concepts, not details. Every assignment handout names the core
concepts it is built around, in its Purpose section. Those are what this
criterion looks at.

A typo, an awkward regex, a rule you could have written more simply — none of
those cost you points here. A misunderstanding of what a `skip` rule does, does.

*What the scores come to:*

Score | Percent | Roughly
-|-|-
9 / 9 | 100%    |	A
8 / 9 |  88.9%  | B+
7 / 9 |  77.8%  | C+
6 / 9 |  66.7%  | D+
5 / 9 |  55.6%  | F
